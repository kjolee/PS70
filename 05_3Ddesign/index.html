(It didn't go very well for Lynn and I) <hr style="border: 1px solid #b33b72;"> <h4>Assignment 1: Design and 3D print a small object </h4> The first assignment of this week was to design and print a small object that could not be easily made by subtractive methods, which would be laser cutting, etc. I decided to design a little Minecraft creeper because my friend from home, Zuni, really likes Minecraft, and I was thinking of her. <div style="display:flex; justify-content:center; align-items:flex-start; gap:20px;"> <figure style="display:inline-flex; flex-direction:column; align-items:center;"> <div style="display:flex;"> <img src="zuni1.JPG" style="height:30vh; width:auto;"> <img src="zuni2.JPG" style="height:30vh; width:auto;"> </div> <figcaption style="font-size:15px; text-align:center;">Zuni loves creepers. I scaled it up by 1.5x so that my figure wouldn't be THAT small. In Fusion, I mainly just made sketches of various squares and rectangles and extruded them to be the height that I wanted. <br></br> <div style="display:flex; justify-content:center; align-items:flex-start; gap:20px;"> <figure style="display:inline-flex; flex-direction:column; align-items:center;"> <div style="display:flex;"> <img src="creeper.png" style="height:30vh; width:auto;"> </div> <figcaption style="font-size:15px; text-align:center;">A Minecraft Creeper</figcaption> </figure> <figure style="display:inline-flex; flex-direction:column; align-items:center;"> <div style="display:flex;"> <img src="dimension.png" style="height:30vh; width:auto;"> </div> <figcaption style="font-size:15px; text-align:center;">Someone on Reddit posted the creeper dimensions</figcaption> </figure> </div> Then, I had trouble figuring out how to attach the legs to the body because I wanted them to be diagonally-angled to make it look like the creeper was walking. Bobby helped me out by showing me the Align tool on Fusion! He also told me to make sure that all the parts were connected better so that they wouldn't be super flimsy after printing (hence why the perpendicular legs have a little extra block bethind them).
