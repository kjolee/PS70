I decided to cut out a cute little flower jewelry holder. This process was SO much easier. If I had gone with this idea in the first place, I would have saved maybe 10+ hours. (Kind of embarassing for me to admit that lol) Anyways, I found an outline of a 5-petal flower online, plopped it into Inkscape, and exported the SVG. Then, I uploaded the SVG into Aspire on the lab computer, set the outside outline to a profile toolpath that was 13.5 mm deep and the inside petals to pocket toolpaths that were 8mm deep. Then, go ShopBot go!
